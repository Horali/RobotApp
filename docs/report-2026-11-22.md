## What the 06:00 sitting actually did

Only TikTok ran. The job `morning-tiktok-0526` started 06:01:36, pushed the clip, walked the composer
and pressed Post at 06:03:58 — then reported `unsure_after_post` because the link read failed. The
session that drove it was cut by an upstream error at 06:12; Instagram and YouTube never started.

## Fixed today (each with a test; suite 146 pass)

1. **The press guard only covered one job** (`8a0a60f`). ClipMaker starts a new job for every sitting,
   and the unique index was `(job_id, upload_id, event)` — so TikTok's Post went out three times
   (21st 07:52, 21st 07:57, 22nd 06:03) and Instagram's Share four times. `claim_press()` now refuses
   any upload that was pressed in *any* earlier job and names that job; `release_press()` is the one
   deliberate way back for a human who has looked and seen nothing was published.
2. **TikTok's link read tapped the Drafts row** (`5faab39`). The fixed point (16 %, 40 %) lands on the
   Drafts entry above the grid. The newest tile is now found on the screen, every tap must prove it
   opened a video page, and an empty profile is reported as "the press published nothing" instead of
   "posted, link not readable".
3. **TikTok's upload confirmation trusted one read** (`5faab39`). A stale hierarchy (the profile from
   step 2) made a composer that never uploaded look done in 1.3 s. It now needs the composer gone on
   three reads in a row, and says so when the composer is still up after 25 s.
4. **Login's missing Profile tab raised the wrong exception** (`28d863c`) — a `RuntimeError` where the
   flow's dead ends raise `ScreenTimeout`; one pre-existing test failure, now green.

## What could still bite tomorrow (YouTube's first real press)

- **The YouTube poster has never posted.** Its dry run reaches the details screen, but the press, the
  upload wait and the link read are unproven. Expect 1–2 selector fixes, as Instagram and TikTok needed.
- Its title row is not exposed to uiautomator2, so the Short goes out with YouTube's default title.
- The upload overlay on YouTube may behave like TikTok's (it returned to the feed in 1.3 s); the
  poster still trusts a single signature. Watch the upload step's duration in the events.
- `_open_newest_video` on TikTok is proven against the fake screen only. The real profile's tile
  bounds are untested — hence the candidate loop with a proof on every tap.
